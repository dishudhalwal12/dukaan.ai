Terms of use

These terms cover ordering from a kirana shop through the Dukaan app or an AI assistant.

Last updated 26 October 2026

Who sells the goods

Each shop on Dukaan is an independent business. Dukaan is software that finds the shop, shows its prices, and passes your order to it. The shop is the seller and the one that delivers.

Orders and prices

Prices and stock come from the shop's own catalogue and are fixed for ten minutes while you confirm. If stock changes in that window, the order fails instead of being adjusted.

A shop can reject an order it cannot fulfil. You can cancel until the shop accepts it. After that, call the shop.

Payment

Payment is cash on delivery. Dukaan does not take payment up front, so there is no refund process for money Dukaan never received.

Delivery

Each shop sets its own delivery radius, usually between one and ten kilometres, and delivers during its own hours. A paused shop does not accept new orders.

Using the service fairly

Do not place orders you do not intend to accept, and do not use automated tools to scrape shops or prices. There are rate limits on previews and orders per hour, and they apply to every account.

Availability

Dukaan is in beta. A shop can pause AI orders, and the service can be paused for maintenance. An order already placed stays visible and can still be tracked or cancelled.

Changes

If these terms change, the date at the top changes with them. Questions go to support.
