Privacy

Dukaan connects customers to kirana shops, in the app and through an AI assistant. This page describes what is stored and why. It applies to the app and to the connector at dukaan-ai-hypnexis.vercel.app/mcp.

Last updated 26 October 2026

At a glance

Data collected: email address and a password hash, your name, phone number and delivery address, the map position you choose to share, and the items and status of your orders.

Purposes: to let you order groceries from a nearby shop, to pass the order and delivery details to that shop, and to show you its status.

Recipients: the shop you order from receives your name, phone, address and basket. Supabase provides authentication and database hosting. We do not sell data or share it with anyone else.

Retention: an unused order preview is deleted 24 hours after it expires. A saved position stops being used after 30 days and stays until you remove it. A saved address stays until you delete it. An order stays while your account exists.

Your controls: delete a saved address, remove the saved position on the consent page, or revoke an assistant from your account. Contact support to delete the account itself.

Contact: support@dukan.ai, or the support page.

Account

Signing in uses an email address and a password, or Google. Supabase handles the sign in and stores a hash of the password. Dukaan never receives the password itself, and neither does the assistant.

Delivery details

You type a name, a phone number, and an address line. The shop uses them to deliver and to call you. The maps position attached to an order is the location you allowed in the app, or a pin you shared in the chat.

Location

A position is captured only when you tap Allow location on the consent page, or when you send a map pin in the chat. It is used to find shops that deliver near you. A position older than 30 days is ignored, and the assistant asks for a pincode instead.

You can remove the saved position on the consent page at any time.

Orders

An order stores the items, quantities, prices, the shop, the delivery details, and the status. The shop you order from sees that order. Other shops do not.

Who sees what

The shop sees your name, phone, address, and the basket it has to prepare. The AI assistant sees shop names, prices, the addresses you saved, and your orders. It does not see your password or another customer's data.

Dukaan does not sell data.

Server logs

The connector logs a hashed user id, the tool name, the duration, and whether it succeeded. Names, phone numbers, addresses, and messages are not written to those lines.

Payments

Payment is cash on delivery. Dukaan does not take a card payment and does not store card numbers.

How long we keep it

An unused order preview is deleted 24 hours after it expires. A saved position stops being used after 30 days and stays until you remove it. A saved address stays until you delete it. An order stays while your account exists, because the shop and you may still need it.

Logs keep a hashed user id, the tool name, the duration, and the outcome. They do not keep names, phone numbers, addresses, or messages.

Location is sensitive, so it is opt in

A position is personal data. Dukaan asks for it only after you tap Allow location, tells you what it is for at that moment, and never takes it silently. You can remove it on the consent page, and an assistant loses access to it as soon as you revoke that assistant.

Deleting your data

Delete a saved address in the app, or ask the assistant to delete it. Remove the saved location on the consent page. Revoke an assistant from the connected apps in your account.

For anything else, including deleting the account itself, contact us through the support page.
